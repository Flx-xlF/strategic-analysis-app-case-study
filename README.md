# Terrain: Corporate Communications Strategy, GEO & Enablement in AI Search

> **The Shift:** Modern corporate communications operates across earned media, owned stories, executive channels, and regional stakeholder briefings—historically assuming different audiences consume those channels separately. In zero-click AI search, that separation disappears: answer engines decompose a single question into **5–11 parallel sub-queries**, pulling passages from press coverage, forums, owned pages, and legacy files into one context window at once.

## Enterprise IT Integration Model

In a regulated enterprise operating across central and regional hubs, this system connects to existing layers rather than running as standalone "shadow IT":

1. **Outside-In:** External feeds—licensed media & social aggregators (**Factiva, Meltwater, Talkwalker**) plus public LLM APIs—ingest massive article streams, digest and transform them into structured data, and process them statistically and with LLMs to identify where messages come through and where work needs to be done.
2. **Top-Down:** Central Comms administers key narratives and GEO frameworks, translates them into **prompt recipes**, and streams them through existing systems straight into day-to-day workplace tools (**Word, Excel, Outlook**) so friction stays minimal and teams benefit immediately.

---

## Realistic Limits

* **No "one-shot, ready-to-send" output:** An LLM will not produce a finished, ready-to-send statement or corporate story in a single pass.
* **Context in, human iteration out:** AI enforces the GEO framework and tailors narratives while surfacing Outside-In communication risks—so human communicators can iterate and finalize the text with full context.
